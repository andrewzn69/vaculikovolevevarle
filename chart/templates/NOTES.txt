Discord Bot has been deployed!

To check the status of the deployment:

  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Values.namespace }}

To view bot logs:

  kubectl logs -n {{ .Values.namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/component=bot -f

To view lavalink logs:

  kubectl logs -n {{ .Values.namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/component=lavalink -f

Enjoy your bot.
